## Short description
The coffee system for web3 events.

## Long description
Beach Coffee Co. runs the coffee bar at Island DAO in Florianópolis, and this is its app.

• See the live menu: what's on, what's in each drink, and whether the bar is open.
• Order from your seat on Telegram and get a ping when your drink is ready. No more waiting in line.
• Tip the barista in USDC on Solana, straight from your phone's wallet with Mobile Wallet Adapter. Works with the Seeker's wallet, Phantom and Solflare.
• Buy the limited Island DAO Edition coffee bag (250 g arabica) in USDC.

Every payment carries a Solana Pay reference and is verified onchain in seconds. No custody: your USDC goes straight to the bar's wallet. Fees are cents, so even a 1 USDC tip makes sense.

2k+ coffees served in one month during Island DAO, built from zero at the bar.
