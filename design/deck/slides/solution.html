Tap, sip, tip. The guest orders a free drink from a Telegram Mini App, where the event already lives. The barista runs every order, Telegram and walk-up, from one panel, and one tap on Ready pings the guest. Then, optionally, the guest tips in USDC on Solana, and the bot confirms it onchain in seconds. LIVE DEMO on the phone, about 60 seconds, leave this slide up: open the bot, tap Order, pick a Latte; it appears on the barista panel; tap Ready and show the ping. Then the tip pop-up: tap 5 USDC, Pay with Phantom, confirm; show the 💛 Tip in the barista’s chat and the transaction on Solscan.
