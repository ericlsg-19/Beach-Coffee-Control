Tip the barista in USDC

Second half of the demo. Open Tip & Bag, choose 5 USDC, Pay with Phantom. The wallet opens our pay page, which builds the USDC transfer; the guest only confirms. Within seconds the barista’s chat gets “💛 Tip”, and the guest sees Thank you. Then show the transaction on Solscan. The same page sells the Island DAO limited-edition coffee bag: 20 bags at 10 USDC, with Sold out when they’re gone. Any other wallet or exchange gets the plain address and amount, each with a Copy button.
