## About

```
Beach Coffee Co. runs the coffee bar at the Island DAO Vila in Floripa.

Guests order free drinks from a Telegram Mini App and get a ping when they're ready. The barista runs the bar from one panel that merges Telegram and walk-up orders, with a live queue, daily reports and a coffee lovers ranking.

Tips and the limited Island DAO coffee bag (20 bags, 10 USDC) are paid in USDC on Solana. Each checkout carries a Solana Pay reference, so the bot confirms the payment onchain within seconds and pings the bar, with no custody and no wallet setup for the shop.

Where it's at: live at the bar. On 27 Oct we served 97 drinks, 17 of them ordered on Telegram.

Looking for: people to try a USDC tip, feedback on the flow, and other events or cafés that want to run it.
```
