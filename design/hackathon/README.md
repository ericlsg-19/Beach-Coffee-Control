## About

```
Coffee, onchain. Proof of brew.

Beach Coffee Co. runs the coffee bar at the Island DAO Vila in Floripa. The whole system was built from zero by the people behind the bar, not a dev team: coded with AI between shifts, shipped to production and used by real guests the same week. Proof that in an ecosystem like this, anyone can build something real.

How it works: guests order free drinks from a Telegram Mini App and get a ping when they're ready. The barista runs the bar from one panel that merges Telegram and walk-up orders, with a live queue, daily reports and a coffee lovers ranking.

Tips and the limited Island DAO coffee bag (20 bags, 10 USDC) are paid in USDC on Solana. Each checkout carries a Solana Pay reference, so the bot confirms the payment onchain within seconds and pings the bar, with no custody.

Where it's at: live at the bar. 356 drinks served from 22 to 28 Oct. Telegram ordering started on 27 Oct and 33 guests already use it; four in five orders now come through Telegram.

Looking for: people to try a USDC tip, feedback on the flow, and other events or cafés that want to run it.
```
